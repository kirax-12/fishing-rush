FISHING RUSH

Mouse / touch: click the water to cast, click fast when a fish bites, then press & hold to reel. Keyboard: hold Space the same way.

How to play

  1. Cast — click, tap, or press Space.
  2. Wait — a shadow fish is circling your bobber…
  3. Strike! — when the ! pops, click again fast or it escapes.
  4. Reel — hold to raise the pink marker, release to let it sink. Keep it inside the green zone to gain progress.
  5. Don't snap the line — if the marker pins at either end, strain builds. Full strain = broken line.
